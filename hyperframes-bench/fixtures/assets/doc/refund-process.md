# Customer Refund Process (internal)

1. Customer requests a refund within 30 days from the Orders page.
2. Support verifies the order is eligible (unused, within window).
3. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method in 3–5 business days.
4. Partial refunds apply a prorated amount for partially-used subscriptions.
5. The customer receives an email confirmation at each step.
