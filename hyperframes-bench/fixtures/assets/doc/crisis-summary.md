# The 2008 Financial Crisis — summary

A housing bubble fueled by subprime mortgages and lax lending burst in 2007–2008. Mortgage-backed securities and derivatives spread the risk across the global financial system. When home prices fell and defaults rose, major institutions (Lehman Brothers, Bear Stearns, AIG) faced insolvency. Credit froze, stock markets crashed, and governments intervened with bailouts (TARP) and emergency rate cuts, triggering the Great Recession.
