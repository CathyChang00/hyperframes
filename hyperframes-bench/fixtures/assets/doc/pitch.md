# MealMate — one-paragraph pitch

MealMate is a meal-planning app that turns your dietary goals and what's already in your fridge into a week of recipes and an auto-sorted grocery list. Pick a goal (lose weight, eat cheaper, go vegetarian), snap your pantry, and MealMate plans every meal, balances macros, and reorders staples before you run out. No site yet — this brief is the whole product.
